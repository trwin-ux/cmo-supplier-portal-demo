Dear Valued Sellers,

To upload new products to your existing stores on City Mall Online, sellers are required to complete the Product Template provided by City Mall Online.

Please ensure that all required product details are accurately filled in according to the relevant product information.

5.1 Product Image Requirements

  • Rename each product image file according to its corresponding product name.
  • Product images may be submitted individually or combined into a ZIP file.
  • All product images must follow the required standard specifications:
    • Image size: 1000 × 1000 pixels
    • Background: White

5.2 Processing Time

Once the completed product information and images have been submitted, the City Mall Online Team will process the request within five (5) working days, in accordance with the established Standard Operating Procedures (SOP).

Sellers are kindly requested to follow the designated SOP and submit all required product information to ensure smooth and timely product uploads.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Sellers who wish to make changes to product prices or stock quantities must notify City Mall Online at least five (5) working days in advance.

Upon receiving the update file from sellers, the CMO Seller Support Team will process the requested changes within five (5) working days, in accordance with the established Standard Operating Procedures (SOP).

All price and stock update requests must be submitted using the designated file format provided by City Mall Online.

Effective from the date of this announcement, sellers are required to manage all return processes directly through the Merchant Dashboard.

2.1 Accessing Returned Orders

Sellers can view returned or cancelled products in the Return QC section, highlighted in red, within the Merchant Dashboard.

This section displays products that have been returned following delivery attempts or cancelled for various reasons.

To inspect cancelled products, click the Return QC button. You will then be redirected to the Return QC In Progress page.

If the QC inspection has not yet been completed, the status will remain as Processing.

2.2 Return Product Inspection

Sellers must physically inspect all returned products to check for any damage, defects, or other issues.

  • If all returned products are in good condition, click the QC Checked button, which will appear in green.
  • Once the QC inspection is completed, click the Finish button to finalize the process.
  • Sellers can also review the cancellation reasons provided by City Mall Online.
  • All inspection details must also be recorded and updated in the Merchant Return Record Form.

2.3 Seller Responsibilities

From the effective date of this announcement, sellers are responsible for checking and managing order acceptance and Return QC activities directly through the Merchant Dashboard.
